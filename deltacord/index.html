1. Who operates DeltaCord

The DeltaCord maintainers determine why and how information is processed for the hosted bot and act as the operator or data controller where applicable. The easiest way to contact the maintainers is through the support method or GitHub repository linked from DeltaCord’s Discord application profile.

2. Information we process

The exact information depends on the features you use. DeltaCord may process the following categories:

Category Examples Why it is needed
Discord identifiers User ID, server ID, channel ID, interaction ID, and application entitlement identifiers. To associate commands and saved game state with the correct user or server and prevent cross-user interaction abuse.
Display information Username, server nickname, avatar URL, or server name as provided by Discord. To display profiles, duel invitations, battle panels, leaderboards, and readable logs. Some values may be fetched live instead of stored.
Gameplay profile Vessel/class, level, XP, region, prestige, gold, inventory, equipment, unlocks, statistics, settings, cooldowns, and achievements. To save progression and operate battles, shops, inventory, classes, and other RPG features.
Duel information Challenges, opponents, turns, health, actions, mutual-mercy state, wins, losses, draws, rating, and match outcomes. To run PvP matches, enforce turns, resolve mercy offers, calculate ratings, and detect abuse.
Interaction and support data Slash-command names, component selections, timestamps, support requests, reports, and information you deliberately submit. To respond to commands, provide support, investigate bugs or abuse, and improve reliability.
Technical and security logs Error messages, stack traces, shard or server state, command failures, rate-limit events, and audit records. To diagnose faults, protect the Service, prevent fraud, and maintain availability.
Premium entitlement data Discord SKU ID, entitlement ID, subscription or purchase status, user/server association, and expiration status. To provide and verify optional paid benefits. We do not receive your full card number.

3. Information we do not intentionally collect

  • DeltaCord is designed around slash commands and Discord interactions and does not intentionally store ordinary message content.
  • The bot does not receive your IP address from Discord.
  • We do not request Discord passwords, account tokens, two-factor codes, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or precise location.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as health information, political beliefs, religion, ethnicity, sexual orientation, or biometric identifiers.
  • We do not use Discord API data for targeted advertising, data-broker profiling, employment, credit, insurance, housing, or similar eligibility decisions.

Do not submit sensitive information through command text, support messages, custom names, or reports. If we receive personal data that is not needed, we may delete it.

  • respond to support, access, correction, and deletion requests;
  • detect exploits, automation, spam, fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • debug errors, measure aggregate reliability, and improve features; and
  • comply with law, Discord requirements, and valid legal requests.

We do not sell or license Discord API data. We do not disclose it to ad networks or data brokers.

6. Storage and retention

Depending on the production configuration, mutable player profiles are stored using either:

  • a local JSON profile store; or
  • a MySQL database configured by the operator.

When a complete MySQL configuration is enabled, the production bot is intended to use MySQL instead of the JSON player store. Static area, monster, item, and shop definitions may remain in JSON and normally do not contain personal data.

We retain active profile data while it is reasonably needed to provide gameplay, maintain integrity, resolve disputes, enforce rules, or comply with law. We may delete inactive profiles or old logs when they are no longer necessary.

After a verified deletion request, we aim to delete or irreversibly anonymize covered active profile data within 30 days. Residual copies may remain in restricted backups for up to 90 days before normal rotation, unless a longer period is legally required or necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Global profile notice: DeltaCord progression may follow your Discord user ID across multiple servers. Removing the bot from one server or leaving that server does not necessarily erase the global profile.

  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or respond to valid legal process.
  • Project transfer: if operation of DeltaCord is transferred, relevant data may move to the successor subject to this Policy or notice of materially different terms.
  • With your direction: we may share information when you explicitly request or authorize it.
